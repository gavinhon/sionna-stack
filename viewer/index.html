What am I looking at?
  • Red dot: the transmitter (TX). In Moving TX it drives along the street and leaves a red trail.
  • White circles: the receivers (phones at 1.5 m). Click one for its numbers.
  • Coloured ground: signal strength at 1.5 m height, using the legend below (path gain in dB, or received power in dBm for realistic transmitters): yellow = strong, green = medium, blue/purple = weak. No colour = no signal reached that spot (inside a building or in deep shadow). With several sectors/beams it shows the best one at each spot.
  • Lines: the routes the signal takes from the TX to a receiver (ray paths). Colour = which receiver (see the swatches under "Ray paths"). Solid and thick = strong path, faint and thin = weak path. A straight line is line of sight; a kinked line bounced off a wall or bent around an edge. Click a line for its details.
  • Buildings: what the simulation used (from OpenStreetMap), coloured by material (glass, stone, metal, concrete; see the key under Layers). Make them more see-through with "building opacity".
  • Chart (Moving TX): total signal at each receiver over the 30 s drive; dots = line of sight.

Tips: Top view shows the coverage like a map. Drag = rotate, scroll = zoom, right-drag = tilt.
