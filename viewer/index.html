What am I looking at?

  • Red dot: a transmitter site. In Moving TX it drives along the street and leaves a red trail.
  • White circles: receivers (phones at 1.5 m). Click one for its numbers.
  • Coloured ground: signal strength at 1.5 m (legend at the top): yellow = strong, green = medium, blue/purple = weak, no colour = no signal reached that spot. With several sectors or beams it shows the best one.
  • Lines: the routes the signal takes to a receiver (ray paths). Colour = which receiver; solid and thick = strong, faint and thin = weak. Straight = line of sight, kinked = bounced off a wall or bent around an edge.
  • Buildings: what the simulation used (OpenStreetMap), coloured by material (key in the Scene tab).
  • Shells (3-D tab): see-through surfaces enclosing the air where the signal is at least a level. Only the boundary is drawn, so empty space inside means strong.
  • Chart (Moving TX): total signal at each receiver over the drive; dots = line of sight.

Drag = rotate, scroll = zoom, right-drag = tilt. Top view (Scene tab) shows the coverage like a map.
